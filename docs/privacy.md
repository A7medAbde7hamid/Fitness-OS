# Privacy Policy - AI Fitness OS

Last updated: August 2026

## Overview

AI Fitness OS is a personal fitness and nutrition tracking application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

## Data We Collect

### Profile Information
- **What**: Display name, email, date of birth, gender, height
- **Why**: To provide personalized fitness recommendations
- **Retention**: Until account deletion

### Weight & Body Metrics
- **What**: Weight measurements, body fat percentage (if provided)
- **Why**: To track progress toward fitness goals
- **Retention**: Until account deletion

### Nutrition Data
- **What**: Logged meals, calories, macronutrients (protein, carbs, fat)
- **Why**: To track dietary intake and provide nutrition coaching
- **Retention**: Until account deletion

### Activity Data
- **What**: Workouts, steps, active minutes, exercise types
- **Why**: To track physical activity and provide workout recommendations
- **Retention**: Until account deletion

### AI Conversations
- **What**: Messages with the AI fitness coach
- **Why**: To provide personalized coaching and maintain conversation context
- **Retention**: Until account deletion

### Meal Images
- **What**: Photos of meals for nutrition analysis
- **Why**: To analyze food content and estimate nutritional values
- **Retention**: Processed in real-time, not stored permanently on our servers

### WhatsApp Connection
- **What**: WhatsApp phone number, message history with the bot
- **Why**: To provide WhatsApp as a communication channel
- **Retention**: Until disconnection or account deletion

### Notification Preferences
- **What**: Enabled/disabled notifications, delivery preferences
- **Why**: To send relevant reminders and updates
- **Retention**: Until account deletion

## How We Use Your Data

1. **Service Provision**: Core functionality (tracking, coaching, analysis)
2. **Personalization**: AI-powered recommendations based on your data
3. **Improvement**: Anonymized usage patterns to improve the app
4. **Communication**: Service updates and important notifications

## Data Storage & Security

- **Database**: Supabase (PostgreSQL) with Row Level Security (RLS)
- **Encryption**: Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
- **Access**: Only you can access your data (enforced by RLS)
- **Backups**: Automated daily backups with point-in-time recovery

## AI & Data Processing

- AI features use Google Gemini API
- Your data is sent to AI only when you actively use AI features
- AI conversations are stored to maintain context
- No data is used to train AI models
- AI responses are generated based on your personal data

## Data Sharing

We do **not** sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties except:

1. **Service Providers**: Supabase (database), Google (AI) - necessary for core functionality
2. **Legal Requirements**: If required by law
3. **With Your Consent**: If you explicitly authorize sharing

## Your Rights

### Access
You can view all your data in the app dashboard.

### Export
Export your data as JSON via Profile → Export Health Data.

### Deletion
Delete your account via Profile → Data Management. This will:
- Remove all your data from our database
- Disconnect WhatsApp (if connected)
- Sign you out immediately
- Data cannot be recovered after deletion

### Correction
Update your profile information anytime via Profile settings.

## Cookies & Local Storage

- **Essential**: Authentication tokens, preferences
- **Functional**: Language settings, unit preferences
- **Cache**: Offline data sync, PWA assets

No third-party tracking cookies are used.

## Children's Privacy

AI Fitness OS is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be communicated via the app.
